⚖️ Advocacy, Advice & Life Planning

Helping individuals and families access the support, funding and opportunities they deserve.

Navigating healthcare, education, social care and funding systems can be overwhelming. From applying for an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) to understanding disability benefits, preparing for a tribunal or planning for the future, many individuals and families face complex processes that can be difficult, time-consuming and emotionally demanding.

At Spectrum, we believe that no one should be disadvantaged because they are unable to navigate these systems alone or cannot afford the high costs often associated with specialist legal and consultancy services.

About This Service

Our Advocacy, Advice & Life Planning service provides specialist advocacy, case support, document preparation and system navigation, helping individuals and families understand their rights, organise evidence, prepare applications and confidently engage with education, health and social care services.

Where regulated legal advice or legal representation is required, we work alongside trusted solicitors and specialist legal professionals to ensure individuals receive the expert support they need, while remaining by their side throughout the process.

Our aim is simple: to remove barriers, unlock opportunities and empower individuals and families to access the support, funding and services they are entitled to, with compassionate, expert guidance every step of the way.

Education Advocacy

Education should provide opportunities, not barriers. We work alongside families and education providers to help ensure children and young people receive the support they are legally entitled to.

Support includes:

Exam Access Arrangements

We help ensure children, young people and university students receive the reasonable adjustments they need to demonstrate their abilities fairly.

Support includes:

Mediation & Tribunal Support

When disagreements arise, families often feel overwhelmed and unsupported. We provide guidance and advocacy throughout the mediation and tribunal process by helping families understand procedures, organise evidence and prepare documentation.

Support may include:

  • Statement preparation

  • Liaison with professionals
  • Guidance throughout the appeals process

Where legal representation is required, we work alongside specialist education solicitors and advocates to ensure families receive appropriate legal advice.

Legal Support

Legal Support for Your Child's Future

Navigating the legal side of SEND (Special Educational Needs and Disabilities) can feel like learning a new language. Our team is here to help you decipher the paperwork and secure the support your child deserves.

“We turn legal jargon into clear steps, so you can focus on being a parent.”

EHCP & Education Support

The EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) process is often long and complex. We provide expert guidance on every document you will face.

Help with the initial request for an Education, Health and Care assessment from your Local Authority. We guide you through the evidence required and what to include to maximise your chances of acceptance.
Reviewing the legal wording of a proposed EHCP to ensure it meets your child's needs. A weak plan leads to weak provision — we make sure every section is legally enforceable.
Guidance on how to update your child's plan as they grow and their needs change. Annual reviews are your opportunity to strengthen the EHCP — we make sure you use it well.
Support if you disagree with a decision made by the Local Authority, including help with Tribal Appeal Forms and professional assistance with the paperwork needed for a legal appeal at SEND Tribunal.
Helping you understand the legal rules for naming a specific school in the EHCP, including how to appeal if the Local Authority refuses your preferred school.
Support with the primary document used to apply for Exam Access Arrangements (such as extra time, a scribe or a reader) for students with learning difficulties or disabilities. We also provide Reasonable Adjustments Templates.

Protecting Their Future: Trust Documents

For children with complex needs, planning for the future is a priority. We help you create a “safety net” for when you are no longer around.

Special Needs Trusts

Legal setups that hold money for your child without affecting their right to government benefits. Essential planning for long-term financial security.

Letters of Wishes

A guide for future carers on exactly how you want your child to be looked after — covering routines, preferences, medical needs and much more.

Wills & Inheritance

Ensuring your estate is handled in a way that protects a vulnerable child — avoiding unintended loss of benefits.

Power of Attorney & Deputyship

Guidance on who makes decisions when a child becomes an adult — including Court of Protection Deputyship and Lasting Power of Attorney.
