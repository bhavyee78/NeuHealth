🏡 Supported Living and Independence

Supported Living and Independence

Safe, high-quality accommodation and personalised support that empowers people to live independently, build confidence and achieve their goals.

Arrange an Initial Consultation Make a Referral

About This Service

At Spectrum Supported Living, we believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to live independently, make their own choices and feel truly at home.

Our Supported Living service provides safe, person-centred accommodation alongside tailored support that enables individuals to develop the skills, confidence and independence needed to live fulfilling lives within their communities.

Whether someone is taking their first step towards independent living, transitioning from education or residential care, or simply requires ongoing support to maintain their tenancy and wellbeing, our experienced team works alongside each individual to help them achieve meaningful and lasting outcomes.

Our Impact

Our Impact

Breaking Cycles of Dependency

By empowering young people with the skills and confidence to live independently, we break cycles of dependency and foster long-term well-being for entire families.

Easing the Family Burden

From guiding individuals through the legal system to offering practical help with meal prep and emotional support, we ease the burden on families and create a foundation for growth.

Helping People Thrive

Our unique combination of life skills training, financial literacy and legal advocacy doesn't just help people survive — it helps them thrive and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Who We Support

Who We Support

We provide supported living services for young adults and adults aged 18 and over, including individuals with:

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
  • Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)
  • Learning disabilities
  • Physical disabilities
  • Mild to moderate mental health needs
  • Acquired brain injuries
  • Sensory impairments
  • Complex social care needs
  • Individuals transitioning from children's services
  • Individuals moving on from residential care
  • Individuals seeking greater independence within their community

Personalised Support

Personalised Support

Every individual receives a personalised support plan designed around their strengths, goals and aspirations. Support may include:

Daily Living Skills

Helping your day-day run smoothly.

  • Developing daily routines
  • Meal planning and preparation
  • Shopping and budgeting
  • Household management
  • Laundry and domestic tasks
  • Time management
  • Building healthy routines

Independence

We support individuals to gain the confidence and practical skills needed to live independently.

This may include:

  • Travel training
  • Managing appointments
  • Community participation
  • Developing social networks
  • Building confidence
  • Decision-making
  • Problem-solving
  • Digital skills
  • Communication skills

Education, Employment & Meaningful Occupation

We believe independence includes having purpose.

We support individuals to:

  • Access education
  • Attend college or university
  • Secure employment
  • Access volunteering opportunities
  • Develop work readiness skills
  • Explore hobbies and interests
  • Build meaningful daily routines

Health & Wellbeing

Maintaining good physical and emotional wellbeing is central to independent living.

Our team supports individuals to:

  • Attend healthcare appointments
  • Build healthy lifestyles
  • Improve emotional wellbeing
  • Develop positive coping strategies
  • Access specialist health services where appropriate
  • Maintain routines that promote overall wellbeing

Positive Behaviour Support

Where appropriate, our support is informed by Positive Behaviour Support (PBS) principles.

We work collaboratively with individuals, families and professionals to understand behaviours, identify strengths and develop proactive strategies that improve quality of life while promoting dignity, choice and independence.

Transition to Adulthood

Moving into adulthood can be challenging for many young people and families.

Our transition support helps individuals successfully move from children's services into adult life by developing practical life skills, increasing confidence and preparing for independent living.

Support is tailored to each person's goals, ensuring they are equipped for the next stage of their journey.

Our Approach

Our Person-Centred Approach

Every individual is unique.

That's why we take the time to understand each person's aspirations, strengths, preferences and support needs before developing a personalised plan that evolves as they grow.

We work closely with families, commissioners and professionals to ensure support remains flexible, responsive and outcome-focused.
