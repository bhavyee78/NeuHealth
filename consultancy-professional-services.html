Specialist Consultancy, Clinical Expertise & Workforce Solutions

At Spectrum, we partner with schools, Multi-Academy Trusts, colleges, universities, healthcare providers, local authorities, employers and other organisations to deliver specialist consultancy, clinical expertise and workforce solutions that improve services, strengthen teams and deliver better outcomes.

We understand that many organisations face increasing demand, workforce shortages and growing waiting lists while striving to provide high-quality, inclusive services. Our flexible partnership model enables organisations to access experienced specialists, multidisciplinary teams and strategic consultancy without the cost, complexity and time associated with permanent recruitment.

Our Services

Our Services

In-House Clinical Assessors

Expand your clinical capacity by embedding experienced specialists directly within your organisation. Rather than recruiting permanent staff, organisations can commission Spectrum to provide dedicated assessors who work as part of your existing team, delivering high-quality services under a flexible partnership model.

We can provide:

  • ADHD Assessors
  • Autism Assessors
  • Dyslexia Assessors
  • Neurodevelopmental Screening Specialists
  • Post-Diagnostic Support Clinicians
  • Specialist Case Review Services
  • Clinical Consultation and Advice

Services can be delivered:

  • On-site
  • Remotely
  • Through hybrid delivery models
  • On a sessional, retained or long-term basis

In-House Multidisciplinary Teams (MDTs)

Build your own multidisciplinary team through Spectrum. Our experienced professionals integrate seamlessly into your organisation, providing coordinated, person-centred support while working alongside your existing staff and leadership teams.

Our MDTs can include:

  • Clinical Psychologists
  • Educational Psychologists (where available)
  • Occupational Therapists
  • Speech and Language Therapists
  • Mental Health Nurses
  • ADHD Specialists
  • Autism Specialists
  • Specialist Teachers
  • Behaviour Specialists
  • Counsellors
  • Psychotherapists
  • Family Support Practitioners
  • Positive Behaviour Support Practitioners

Whether you require one specialist or a fully integrated MDT, we provide flexible solutions tailored to your operational and clinical needs.

For Education Providers

Your Partner in SEN & Legal Compliance

With NHS waiting lists at an all-time high and new laws coming into play, educational settings are often left to support students without a formal diagnosis. At Spectrum Neuro Health, we provide a bridge between clinical excellence and legal safety.

Student Referrals

Guide your students directly to us for ADHD, Autism or Dyslexia assessments. We provide a full 360-degree view, including classroom observations and staff interviews.

In-House Assessors

Hire an expert (ADHD, Autism or Dyslexia specialist) to work directly in your school for a day or a week. We offer flat daily rates for in-house work.

MDT Support

Access a full Multi-Disciplinary Team of specialists for complex cases — all in one place, for one fixed price. No need to coordinate multiple providers.

Legal Support

Help senior leaders draft and review SEND policies to ensure legal compliance. We know the law — and we help you stay on the right side of it.

EHCP Quality Audits

Our legal team reviews your school's EHCPs to make sure they are legally strong, clearly written and easy for staff to follow.

Staff Training

Practical workshops for teachers and teaching assistants on how to better support SEND learners in the classroom. Not every child needs an EHCP to deserve support.

The Benefits for Your School

Clear the Backlog

Better Parent Relations

Cost-Effective
