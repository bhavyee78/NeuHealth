🏡 Home & Community Support

Specialist support for young adults, adults and older people to live independently, stay connected and thrive in their community.

Our Home & Community Support service provides practical, person-centred assistance for people who need additional support at home or in everyday life but do not require personal care. We support individuals with autism, ADHD, learning disabilities, mental health needs, physical disabilities, dementia and age-related conditions, helping them build confidence, maintain routines and remain engaged with their communities. Our services are for people of all ages 18+.

Young adults entering adulthood

People with autism and/or ADHD

People with learning disabilities

People experiencing mental health challenges

Older adults who need companionship and practical support

People living with dementia or Alzheimer's disease

Family carers seeking reliable respite and support

Our Support

What We Can Help With

At Home

  • Daily routines and structure
  • Meal preparation
  • Shopping and errands
  • Household organisation
  • Budgeting and money management
  • Support attending appointments

In the Community

  • Travel training and public transport support
  • Social and community activities
  • Education, college and employment support
  • Confidence building and independence skills
  • Family respite and companionship support

Important Information

At present, this service provides non-regulated home and community support only. We do not currently provide personal care, including assistance with washing, dressing, toileting or medication administration, while we complete our CQC registration process.
