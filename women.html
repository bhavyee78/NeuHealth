About This Service

Specialist neurodevelopmental healthcare, wellbeing and advocacy designed around the unique experiences of women and girls.

Many women spend years asking themselves, “Why do I feel different?”

For some, the answer doesn't come until adulthood. Others spend years being misunderstood, overlooked or treated for anxiety, depression or other conditions before their underlying neurodevelopmental differences are recognised.

At Spectrum, we believe every woman deserves answers, understanding and support, without judgement, without stereotypes and without unnecessary delay.

Our team specialise in women's health and this has been created to recognise the unique ways neurodevelopmental conditions can present in women and girls. Through specialist assessments, personalised healthcare, advocacy and holistic support, we help women better understand themselves, access the right support and build lives where they can thrive with confidence.

Whether you are seeking answers for yourself, supporting your daughter, navigating university, balancing family life or looking for support later in adulthood, our team is here to walk alongside you every step of the way.

Understanding Neurodivergence in Women

Understanding Neurodivergence in Women

Every woman's experience is unique, and that's exactly why specialist understanding matters.

For many years, research into neurodevelopmental conditions focused predominantly on boys and men. As a result, many women and girls have gone undiagnosed, been diagnosed much later in life or received support for the symptoms they experienced rather than the underlying cause.

Many women learn to mask, camouflage or compensate for their differences from an early age. They may appear to cope well on the outside while privately experiencing exhaustion, anxiety, burnout or a lifelong feeling of being different.

Because these experiences often don't fit traditional stereotypes, many women are not identified until adulthood, sometimes after years of searching for answers.

At Spectrum Neuro Health, our clinicians understand these differences. We take time to understand each individual's story, recognising that neurodevelopmental conditions can present differently across childhood, adolescence and adulthood.

Our assessments look beyond checklists and stereotypes, ensuring every woman is seen, heard and understood as a whole person.

A Different Presentation

Neurodivergence Can Present Differently in Women

While every individual is different, women and girls may experience autism, ADHD and other neurodevelopmental conditions in ways that differ from the traditional presentations described in clinical literature.

Some women may:

Mask or camouflage their difficulties to fit social expectations.

Develop coping strategies that hide underlying challenges.

Experience chronic exhaustion from constantly adapting to their environment.

Be perceived as anxious, shy, perfectionistic or overly organised rather than neurodivergent.

Experience emotional overwhelm, burnout or heightened sensory sensitivities.

Find friendships and relationships difficult despite appearing socially confident.

Internalise their difficulties rather than displaying outward behaviours.

Be misdiagnosed with anxiety, depression, eating disorders or personality disorders before receiving an accurate neurodevelopmental assessment.

Reach adulthood before recognising that lifelong challenges may be linked to autism or ADHD.

Recognising these patterns is essential to ensuring women receive accurate assessments, meaningful support and the understanding they deserve.

Post-Diagnostic Support

Post-Diagnostic Support

Receiving a diagnosis is often the beginning of a new chapter, not the end of the journey.

Following assessment, we provide personalised support to help women understand their diagnosis, recognise their strengths and develop practical strategies that improve everyday life.

Support may include:

  • Understanding your diagnosis
  • Psychoeducation
  • One-to-one coaching
  • Emotional wellbeing support
  • Lifestyle planning
  • Parent and family guidance
  • Workplace and education support
  • Goal setting
  • Community signposting
  • Ongoing clinical support

Our focus is not simply on diagnosis, but on helping women build confidence, improve wellbeing and move forward with clarity.

Women's Health & Wellbeing

Women's Health & Wellbeing

Supporting every aspect of a woman's health, not just a diagnosis. Neurodevelopmental health is influenced by many factors throughout life.

Hormonal changes, menstruation, pregnancy, parenthood, menopause, trauma, chronic stress, and major life transitions are some of the things that are affected when you are a Neurodivergent woman.

Our clinicians understand that these experiences matter.

That's why our specialist clinicians consider the whole person, providing personalised care, allows us to reflect each woman's unique circumstances, health & wellbeing goals

Our wellbeing services may include:

  • Personalised wellbeing programmes
  • Lifestyle coaching
  • Emotional wellbeing support
  • Confidence and self-esteem development
  • Stress and burnout management
  • Healthy routines and self-care strategies
  • Goal setting
  • Life transition support
  • Family and relationship guidance
  • Community wellbeing programmes

Community, Connection & Belonging

Community, Connection & Belonging

Feeling understood can be life-changing.

Many women describe feeling isolated before discovering they are neurodivergent. Building connections with others who share similar experiences can reduce loneliness, increase confidence and provide valuable peer support.

Spectrum is committed to creating safe, welcoming communities where women can connect, learn and grow together.

Through workshops, wellbeing programmes, community initiatives and future events, we aim to ensure no woman has to navigate her journey alone.

Your Health. Your Safety. Your Future.

Your Health. Your Safety. Your Future.

Because every woman deserves to feel safe, understood and empowered.

At Spectrum, safeguarding extends beyond policies and procedures.

We recognise that many neurodivergent women experience increased vulnerability throughout their lives, whether through social isolation, burnout, exploitation, challenges accessing healthcare or difficulties navigating education, employment and relationships.

Our commitment is to create environments where women feel listened to, respected and supported.

We work collaboratively with healthcare professionals, families, community organisations and specialist services to ensure women have access to the care, information and advocacy they need to protect their health, promote their wellbeing and make informed decisions throughout every stage of life.

Why Choose Spectrum

Why Choose Spectrum?

Women choose Spectrum because we understand that no two journeys are the same.

Our integrated model combines specialist healthcare, social care, advocacy and wellbeing services under one organisation, allowing women to access coordinated support without repeatedly navigating multiple systems.

By bringing together clinical expertise, personalised care and lifelong support, we help women gain clarity, build confidence and create futures where they can thrive.
